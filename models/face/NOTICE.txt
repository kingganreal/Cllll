Face models from InsightFace (https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface), "buffalo_s" pack (v0.7):
  det_500m.onnx   SCRFD-500M face detector (with 5 face landmarks)
  w600k_mbf.onnx  MobileFaceNet face recognition, trained on WebFace600K

The InsightFace pretrained models are available for non-commercial research purposes only.
